# Refund and Replacement Policy

1. Customers may receive a full refund when they request it within 30 calendar days of purchase and provide proof of purchase.
2. After 30 days, a customer-support agent cannot approve or promise a refund.
3. A defective product is eligible for a replacement within 90 calendar days of purchase.
4. A customer-support agent may escalate an out-of-policy refund request to a billing specialist.
5. The billing specialist will respond within two business days.
6. An escalation does not guarantee that the refund will be approved.
