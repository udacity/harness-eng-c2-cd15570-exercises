# Storyboard — Video 5: Operate and Evolve with Evidence

**Target runtime:** 7 minutes  
**Visual motif:** A verification pyramid transitions into a responsibility
comparison and operational feedback loop.

## Slide 1 — Verification portfolio

**Time:** 0:00–0:50  
**Visual:** Pyramid: parse/import, validation, units, local integration, live
scenario, post-run audit.  
**Callout:** Use the lowest deterministic layer that can prove the claim.

## Slide 2 — Deterministic coverage

**Time:** 0:50–1:40  
**Visual:** Checklist of registration, permission, safety, repeat, completion,
trace, and command-construction tests.

## Slide 3 — Diagnose by missing boundary

**Time:** 1:40–2:30  
**Visual:** Trace pipeline with each absent event pointing to its likely owner:
selection, identity, safety evidence, handler, or oracle.

## Slide 4 — Exercise parity gate

**Time:** 2:30–3:20  
**Visual:** Python loop and Hermes each produce a different client; both feed
the same behavioral test and must pass before comparison.

## Slide 5 — Responsibility comparison

**Time:** 3:20–4:25  
**Visual:** Ownership table: continuation, dispatch, hook order, completion,
trace, and usage.  
**On-screen text:** Compare ownership, not raw line count.

## Slide 6 — Avoid false rankings

**Time:** 4:25–5:05  
**Visual:** Cross out tiny timing delta, different provider, generated-code
style, and LOC as standalone conclusions.

## Slide 7 — Production extensions

**Time:** 5:05–6:10  
**Visual:** Outer operational ring: release stages, identities/secrets,
concurrency, durable evidence, monitoring, redaction, runbooks.

## Slide 8 — Final principle

**Time:** 6:10–7:00  
**Full-screen statement:** “Execute legitimate work. Prevent the wrong work.
Prove the difference.”  
**End card:** Module 6 complete.
